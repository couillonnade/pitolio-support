Pitolio support

For Pitolio 1.2.0, start with the quick start or troubleshooting.

Email pitolio@couillonnade.com for help. You can also use this repository's Issues tab for bug reports and feature requests when the repository is public.

Please include:

  • iPhone or iPad model, iOS/iPadOS version, and Pitolio version from About & tips.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened.
  • Whether the problem involved a book, Pencil, export, Fuel timer or Auto Track.
  • A screenshot if useful. Remove private flight notes and other personal information first.

For Pencil problems, include your Pencil model and whether the page was zoomed or showing two pages. For timer problems, say which fuel mode you used, whether Auto Track was enabled, whether the device was locked, and whether a fuel or time editor was open. For template/copy problems, attach a non-private source example and say whether the pages predate the template change.

The Bug report and Feature request forms ask for these details. GitHub issues are public: do not include private flight notes or other personal information. Use email for private requests.
